Dash

The First Try mascot. Its eyes follow the pointer, it blinks, and it hops when you click it. The buttons switch its mood.

Moods

Each mood stands for a product state: idle (waiting), watching (you are typing), thinking (working), curious (it has a question), done (prompt ready), oops (an error) and asleep (it cannot work yet).

Sizes

At 32 px and under it drops the drips and shadow and its eyes grow.
